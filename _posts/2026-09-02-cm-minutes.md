# Gerrit Community Managers Update: September 2026

*September 2, 2026 / news*

This update covers key discussions, events, and milestones achieved by the
Gerrit Community Managers team since our last update in January, reflecting
recent activities, community initiatives, and upcoming developments.
The meetings have been happening regularly every other week, and while all
events and activity have been announced in due course, updates regarding the
actual meetings have been quite scarce, this article aims to remediate that, so
here is a recap of everything achieved in the last year.

## Team Changes & Welcome Josie

The CM team has evolved again since our last update, as recently announced by
Nasser. We are delighted to welcome Josie as our newest Community Manager! 

Please join us in giving Josie a warm welcome to the team—we look forward to
working together to support and grow the community.

## Website Redesign

The long-awaited [gerritcodereview.com](https://gerritcodereview.com) redesign
has been successfully completed and released! The site now features a cleaner,
more modern look. Head over to check it out—further contributions, feedback, and
improvements are always welcome.

## Gerrit User Summit 2026

Organizing early for this year's summit alongside Google has paid off immensely.
The Call for Papers (CFP) has seen unprecedented engagement, attracting over 40
talk submissions so far—making this our highest number of CFP responses to date. 

Registration is still open, head over to gus26.gerritforge.com to find out more.

Finally, because of the overwhelming response, we are looking to potentially
close the CFP early, in the next couple of weeks, so if you're undecided about
submitting a talk, please do so sooner rather than later. We'll communicate
about any updates to CFP closing date separately.

Thank you to everyone who submitted a proposal and registered to participate.

## Community Hackathon

We’ve been liaising with Nvidia, who have kindly offered to organize and host
this year's community hackathon which is being organized by them. If you want
more details around it please email gerrit-hackathon-2026@nvidia.com.
Huge thanks to Nvidia for stepping up to support the project.

## Gerrit Contributor Hangout

Our regular contributor hangouts continue to run smoothly and spark great
conversations across the ecosystem, thanks Nasser for organasing them.
They’ve proven particularly valuable for welcoming and onboarding new
contributors to projects like the `gerrit mcp server`. 

If you haven't joined one yet, keep an eye out for upcoming events on Discord!

## New Logo Contest

Thanks to Luca, we have officially kicked off a New Logo contest for Gerrit! The
submission and community evaluation phase is underway, and the final outcome
will be defined and shared in due course.

## Spam Prevention Measures

To keep our community channels clean and usable, active discussions are ongoing
regarding new permission structures to curb spam across our main sites. We will
keep you updated as these controls are finalized and implemented.
