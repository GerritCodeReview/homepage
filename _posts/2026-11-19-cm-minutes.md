**Participants**: Dani, Josie, Matthias, Nasser

## Summary
We discussed various things, from the new Gerrit logo, GUS26 feedback and
Gerrit's roadmap representation, to how to make the Discord server more
discoverable.

## Gerrit Logo
Luca launched a survey on the Gerrit logo on
[repo-discuss](https://groups.google.com/g/repo-discuss/c/QRfpRkqhvzA/m/cOCf8c-MAAAJ).
Despite the high participation in the vote, the feedback from the community and
the Community Managers seemed to indicate that none of the proposals really cut
it. Nasser proposed a new design that we intend to refine and then put to the
community's vote again. However, before doing so, Josie will try to figure out
Google's internal process to understand who needs to approve a logo change.

Dani also stressed that, if we are to change the logo, we should ensure that we
only use the one new logo and remove references to Gerrit's current actual
logo, which is the green box with the two "--++" that you see in the favicon.
Diffy is technically the mascot; having two logos is extremely confusing and we
should converge on one.

## GUS26 Feedback
The feedback from this year's User Summit was incredibly positive, with almost
all of the participants enjoying the food and drinks (although coffee and water
ran out a bit too quickly) as well as the spaces that Google hosted us in. Some
feedback suggested that more time for informal networking, and maybe even
targeted unconference-style sessions, would be appreciated! We'll keep this in
mind for next year and see how we can accommodate it in the setting we will
have then.

We briefly talked about the setting for next year. Survey responders expressed a
preference for the SF-Bay area again. Nasser suggested potentially teaming up
with Git Merge; Dani, however, noted that we already tried that this year and
there wasn't much traction from the Git Merge organizers. He also highlighted
how the set-up could be challenging: a separate track for forges wouldn't work,
as there isn't much overlap between them, and a single track for each forge
would be too much. Nasser suggested organizing it as a follow-up event, for
example on the following day. However, if JJ Con starts regularly co-locating
with Git Merge, that's already a 3-day event, and extending it to a 4 or 5-day
event could be too much.

Another option is to team up with the OpenInfra Foundation conference again, as
it was a great experience in Paris in 2025. We'll keep investigating during the
year.

## Gerrit Roadmap
It's challenging for the main teams involved to publicly commit to their Gerrit
roadmap. However, we should aim to at least have long-term objectives
represented there, even if they're not currently committed to. This should at
least give an idea of where the project is headed long term.

## Making the Discord More Discoverable
Dani highlighted how the mailing list has been quite quiet recently, while the
[Discord](https://www.gerritcodereview.com/contact.html#discord) server has
become very busy, which is great to see. Nasser pointed out that we could make
it more discoverable and has already added a link to it in the top section of
the Google Group.

Until next time,

Dani
